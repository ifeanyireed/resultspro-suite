01 · Foundation

Vision & core philosophy

Most learning platforms optimize for content delivery. BuilderOS optimizes for human transformation — the product doesn't sell videos, certificates, or even the underlying UI/UX curriculum. It sells momentum, accountability, community, confidence, and eventually independence.

Not this

Self-paced content library

Upload videos, let learners consume alone, hope completion happens. This is Udemy, Coursera, and every white-label LMS — content-first, human-optional.

This

Mentor-guided, cohort-driven, project-based building

Students don't consume — they build, collaborate, receive feedback, demonstrate progress in public, and become professionals inside a class that knows their name.

"How do we make someone feel like they're walking into a classroom where people know their name, expect their contribution, celebrate their progress, and help them become a builder?" — the single question every screen in BuilderOS must answer.

Portfolio

Real, demonstrable project work — not a certificate of attendance.

Confidence

The primary outcome. Employment and freelancing are downstream of it.

Community

Classmates, mentors, and peers who are visibly, actively present.

Momentum

Streaks, sprints, and check-ins that make stopping feel harder than continuing.

Foundation

Target audience

BuilderOS is built for the working professional squeezing transformation into evenings — not the unemployed full-time bootcamper most EdTech products are designed around.

Who they are

  • → Age 22–40, employed or underemployed
  • → Full-time job, studies evenings and weekends
  • → Based in Lagos and other Nigerian / African urban centers
  • → Has tried self-paced courses before and abandoned them

What they want

  • → Accountability that a YouTube playlist can't give them
  • → A real mentor who knows their work, not a forum thread
  • → Structure — a syllabus with a start, a middle, and a demo day
  • → Practical, portfolio-grade experience over theory
What the UI must make them feel
"I'm expected.""I have classmates.""My mentor knows me." "I'm making progress.""I'm behind — I should continue." "I'm proud.""My work matters.""I belong."
Foundation

Design language

Imagine if Discord, Notion, Apple Fitness, Linear, Duolingo, GitHub, Figma, and a physical classroom had a baby. Not corporate. Not Moodle. Not Blackboard. Not Google Classroom.

White-first

Airy, elegant, minimal. Never clinical.

Soft geometry

Rounded corners, soft shadows, subtle gradients.

Smooth motion

Transitions that reinforce liveness, never decorate for its own sake.

Premium restraint

Beautiful typography, large spacing, no clutter.

02 · People

Roles & responsibilities

Four roles run BuilderOS. Notice the naming: "Instructor" is deliberately absent — the role is Mentor.

Student

The builder

Learns, watches lessons, reads resources, submits projects, joins calls, chats, collaborates, and tracks their own progress.

Mentor

Not "Instructor"

Reviews work, grades, comments, schedules meetings, encourages students, pairs peers, approves milestones, monitors engagement.

Program Admin

Runs the cohort machine

Creates programs, configures cohorts, uploads lessons, configures payments, manages mentors and students, generates reports.

Super Admin

Platform owner

Billing, platform configuration, mentor management, content governance, analytics, CDN, AI features, and permissions.

Product

Core modules

The systems that actually deliver the "mentor operating system" promise — expanded from concept to feature list.

Module structure

Content engine

Every module a student opens follows the same reliable shape, so momentum never breaks on inconsistent formatting.

  • Introduction & objectives
  • Estimated duration
  • Required readings & videos
  • Interactive examples
  • AI summary of the lesson
  • Reflection questions
  • Quiz + mini challenge
  • Project + peer activity
  • Submission + mentor feedback
  • Completion celebration

Content types supported

Authoring

Admins and mentors aren't limited to one format — the player renders whatever the lesson calls for.

  • Markdown & rich text
  • Embedded video (native, YouTube, Loom)
  • Interactive prototypes
  • PDF & slides
  • Audio & code snippets
  • Embedded Figma & Miro
  • Live sessions
  • Downloadable resources

AI features

Differentiator

This is where BuilderOS stops looking like an LMS and starts looking like an operating system — AI removes the mentor-bandwidth ceiling that kills every solo-founder education business.

  • Quiz Generator — mentor uploads a lesson, AI returns MCQ, true/false, scenario, and reflection questions calibrated to Bloom's Taxonomy levels
  • Assignment Generator — mentor sets lesson, difficulty, duration → AI drafts project ideas, acceptance criteria, deliverables, and bonus tasks
  • Rubric Generator — auto-builds grading rubrics from the assignment brief
  • Mentor Assistant — surfaces who's falling behind, improving, at dropout risk, and what to do about it
  • Progress Summary — weekly AI digest of each student's strengths, weaknesses, confidence, and learning velocity

Classroom experience

Presence, not chat

Students should feel that people exist. This is the module that makes the product feel alive minute to minute.

  • Who's online / studying / designing / reviewing / presenting
  • Live mentor availability & typing indicators
  • Study groups, cowork rooms, voice rooms, open discussions

Peer collaboration

Pairing engine

Automatic, manual, mentor, random, skill-based, or timezone-based pairing — students chat, call, review, and co-edit.

  • Shared submission with group grades
  • Structured peer evaluations
  • Comment & co-edit on shared work

Gamification

Professional, not childish

Points, XP, levels, streaks, and badges tuned for adult professionals — momentum without gimmicks.

Consistency streakFast FinisherMentor's Choice Top ReviewerBest TeammateProject Hero Builder LevelWeekly ChampionClass Champion Portfolio CompletePerfect Attendance

Submission & review flow

Student submits a link, file, or repo → version is stored automatically → mentor is notified instantly → mentor approves, rejects, or requests changes with annotation, voice note, or recorded video feedback, all without leaving the page.

Cohort system

Every learner belongs to a cohort with shared photos, attendance, streaks, leaderboard, milestones, upcoming demos, and wins — the social unit the whole product is built around.

Mentor dashboard

An air-traffic-control center: student health, engagement, completion, attendance, risk alerts, pending reviews, weekly wins, students needing encouragement, and AI recommendations — one screen, full situational awareness.

Payments & notifications

Monthly subscriptions with automatic renewal, grace periods, reminders, restricted access on overdue, and automatic restoration on payment — paired with event-driven notifications for every moment that matters (review received, streak hit, demo starting).

Engineering

AI layer

AI exists to remove the single ceiling that kills every mentor-guided education business: one human can only review so much work. Every AI feature routes through a shared orchestrator, not five disconnected integrations.

AI jobTriggerInputOutput
Quiz generationMentor uploads a lessonMarkdown / PDF / slidesMCQ, true/false, scenario & reflection questions with hints and Bloom-level tagging
Assignment generationMentor sets lesson + difficulty + durationStructured briefProject ideas, acceptance criteria, deliverables, timeline, bonus tasks
Rubric generationAssignment createdAssignment briefWeighted grading rubric
Mentor assistantNightly + on-demandCohort activity dataRisk-of-dropout flags, who needs help, recommended interventions
Progress summaryWeekly, per studentSubmissions, quiz scores, attendanceStrengths, weaknesses, confidence, learning velocity

All AI jobs run asynchronously through the ai_jobs queue so a slow generation never blocks a mentor's UI — they get a notification when output is ready to review and edit before it reaches a student.
