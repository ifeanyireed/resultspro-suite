# Projectile Motion, Vector Resolution & Range

## Learning Objectives
1. Resolve initial launch velocity $u$ at angle $\theta$ into horizontal ($u \cos\theta$) and vertical ($u \sin\theta$) components.
2. Derive and apply formulae for Time of Flight ($T$), Maximum Height ($H$), and Horizontal Range ($R$).

## 1. Key Equations of Projectile Motion
- **Time of Flight**: $T = \frac{2u \sin\theta}{g}$
- **Maximum Height**: $H = \frac{u^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g}$
- **Horizontal Range**: $R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$ (Maximum when $\theta = 45^\circ$)

## 2. Exercises
1. A stone is projected at $30\text{ m/s}$ at an angle of $30^\circ$ to the horizontal ($g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$). Calculate: (a) Time of flight, (b) Maximum height, (c) Range.
