# Whole Numbers up to 1,000,000 and Place Value

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week's lesson, pupils in **Primary 4** will be able to:
1. Count, read, and write whole numbers up to $1,000,000$ in numerals and words.
2. Identify the place value of each digit in a 6-digit or 7-digit number.
3. Order and compare whole numbers using $<$, $>$, and $=$.

## 1. Counting in Thousands and Hundreds of Thousands
When we group $10$ hundreds together, we get **one thousand** ($1,000$).
- $10$ thousands $= 10,000$ (Ten thousand)
- $100$ thousands $= 100,000$ (One hundred thousand)
- $10$ hundred thousands $= 1,000,000$ (One million)

## 2. Place Value Table
Consider the number **$648,392$**:

| Hundred Thousands (HT) | Ten Thousands (TT) | Thousands (Th) | Hundreds (H) | Tens (T) | Units (U) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $6$ | $4$ | $8$ | $3$ | $9$ | $2$ |

- The value of **$6$** is $6 \times 100,000 = 600,000$.
- The value of **$4$** is $4 \times 10,000 = 40,000$.
- The value of **$8$** is $8 \times 1,000 = 8,000$.
- The value of **$3$** is $3 \times 100 = 300$.
- The value of **$9$** is $9 \times 10 = 90$.
- The value of **$2$** is $2 \times 1 = 2$.

## 3. Worked Example — Nigerian Market Story
Mama Chidinma sold $245,500$ Naira worth of yams in Mile 12 Market on Monday and $134,250$ Naira on Tuesday.
- In words, $245,500$ is **Two hundred and forty-five thousand, five hundred**.
- The digit $4$ stands for **forty thousand** ($40,000$).

## 4. Practice Exercises
1. Write $582,419$ in expanded form and state the place value of $8$.
2. Write **Seven hundred and sixteen thousand, two hundred and five** in figures.
3. Which is greater: $492,100$ or $429,990$?

## Teacher Guidance
Use bundles of counters or abacus columns to demonstrate carrying over from the Ten-Thousands column into the Hundred-Thousands column.
