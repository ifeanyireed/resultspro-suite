# Algebraic Fractions, LCM & Commercial Word Problems

## Learning Objectives
1. Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of algebraic denominators.
2. Add and subtract algebraic fractions with monomial and binomial denominators.
3. Formulate and solve word problems leading to simple algebraic fractions.

## 1. Adding Algebraic Fractions
To simplify $\frac{2x}{3} + \frac{x}{6}$:
1. The LCM of $3$ and $6$ is $6$.
2. Convert each fraction: $\frac{4x}{6} + \frac{x}{6} = \frac{5x}{6}$.

## 2. Worked Example — Balogun Market Cooperative
A trader invests $\frac{1}{3}$ of her capital in textiles and $\frac{1}{4}$ in footwear. If the total invested in both is $350,000$ Naira, find her total capital $C$:
$$\frac{C}{3} + \frac{C}{4} = 350,000 \implies \frac{7C}{12} = 350,000 \implies C = 600,000\text{ Naira}$$

## 3. Exercises
1. Simplify $\frac{3a - 1}{4} - \frac{a + 2}{6}$.
2. Solve for $y$: $\frac{y - 2}{3} + \frac{y + 1}{2} = 4$.
