The goal is that regardless of whether a school subscribes to only AdmissionsPRO or the full ecosystem, the app still feels complete.

# MOBILE NAVIGATION (V1)

For most users, keep only:

### Bottom Navigation

1. Home
2. Learning
3. Community
4. Notifications
5. Profile

Everything else is accessed through contextual menus and role-based shortcuts.

This gives you roughly **55 screens**, but each user only sees about **10–15 relevant screens**, keeping the experience simple while allowing SchoolHub to scale into a full digital campus platform.
